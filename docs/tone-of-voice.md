# SoopaNova tone of voice

The voice the site uses (October 2026), the rules it follows, the decisions made in its first review, and the audiences to weigh it against. Use it as the guide for new copy.

## In one line

**A gamer who makes prints, talking to other gamers:** playful in the headlines, straight-talking about the product.

## Voice pillars

### 1. Gamer to gamer

Gaming references and in-jokes, used lightly, mostly in headings, taglines and empty states.

| Sounds like | Where |
|---|---|
| "Power-up Prints" | Homepage section, create page |
| "It appears this side quest has been lost to the void." | 404 |
| "Show your 'shot" · "Go big (or go small)" · "Get back to gaming" | How it works |
| "limited by the technology of my time" | About (a meme) |
| "One shot, one print" | Sustainability |

### 2. Short, confident headlines

Few words and a clear promise, often in two beats.

- "From Pixels to Prints."
- "Make something awesome. Make it your own."
- "Upload your screenshots. Create your prints."
- "Less waste, fewer miles"
- "Paper, canvas or frame?"

### 3. Plain and precise about the product

Since the Prodigi re-platform: exact specs, no puffery, and the downsides said out loud. Everything matches Prodigi's own specs and names (see the "accurate product info" rule).

- "No fixings included: it hangs on a nail or screw"
- "Not available in your country"
- "Every print is made to order just for you, so we can't take it back if you simply change your mind."
- "The honest bit" (sustainability): "we don't claim to be carbon neutral"

### 4. Personal and maker-led

The About page is a first-person story: the brother, the Forza screenshot, the papaya-orange McLaren F1. It explains why SoopaNova exists, and the rest of the site borrows its warmth.

- "I noodled on this idea for a while, but never really threw myself into it."
- "TLDR; I love coding, video games & art - therefore SoopaNova was forged."

### 5. British and casual

British spelling and idiom, contractions, the odd mild slang.

- "favourite", "colour", "centimetres", "petrol-head", "mighty-fine", "pretty damn good"

## Style rules already in place

- **British English** spelling and dates.
- **Contractions** everywhere (we'll, can't, it's).
- **"We"** for SoopaNova and its print partner, **"you/your"** for the reader; **"I"** only on About (see decision 7).
- **Eyebrows:** short, mono, uppercase: "PRINTS", "SCREENSHOT TIPS", "SUSTAINABILITY".
- **Hero headings** end with a pink full stop ("From Pixels to Prints.").
- **Sizes** as `14 × 24″` (short edge first) with centimetres alongside; prices in the visitor's currency, never hardcoded.
- **Product and option names** are Prodigi's (Art print, Framed canvas, Antique gold, Mirror wrap).
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do: "We couldn't start checkout. Please try again."
- **No absolute green or quality claims** ("eco-friendly", "zero waste", "highest quality") unless the supplier states them for our products.

## Decisions (October 2026)

The inconsistencies found in the first review, and how they were settled:

1. **Sentence case** for every heading, title and label ("Choose your platform", "Page not found", "Print options"). Proper nouns keep their capitals ("PlayStation App", "Xbox", "Prodigi").
2. **No puffery.** Say what's true and specific ("our print partner, Prodigi … usually in the market it's going to") rather than "the highest quality materials". The one exception we keep for its charm: **"mighty-fine artwork"**.
3. **Two calls to action:** **"Start creating"** (to `/create/`, or straight into the file picker when signed in) and **"Browse prints"** (to `/prints/`). "Read the FAQ" stays as a secondary link where it fits.
4. **Calm error feedback:** no exclamation marks, say what happened and what to do next ("That file is too big. Screenshots can be up to 64 MB."). Exclamations are for good news only, if at all.
5. **"Power-up prints"** is the `/create/` page's eyebrow, with "Start with a screenshot" as its heading. The SEO title stays "Create". The homepage section eyebrow is now "Prints".
6. **Gaming flavour, from popular culture rather than niche references,** where it was missing:
   - FAQ: "It's dangerous to go alone. Take these answers." (Zelda)
   - `/prints`: "Choose your fighter" (fighting games)
   - Screenshot tips: "Pro tips"
   - Product pages: "Ready, player one?" before "Start creating"
   - Empty orders page: "Your inventory's empty for now."
   - Order confirmation: "Achievement unlocked: order placed"

   Keep it to headings, taglines, empty states and confirmations; never in specs, prices, legal text or errors.
7. **"I" and "we":** SoopaNova is one person. The About story stays in the first person, and it now says so: "SoopaNova is just me. When the rest of the site says 'we', that's me and my print partner, Prodigi, who print and post every order." Everywhere else, "we" means SoopaNova and Prodigi together.

## Audiences to weigh it against

These are working assumptions to review, not research.

| Audience | What they want | What lands | Risks in today's voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Virtual photographers:** photo-mode enthusiasts sharing shots online | Print quality, resolution, colour, crop control | Exact specs, DPI grading, the 4K table, "Hide the HUD" | Jokey headlines can read as less serious about quality |
| **Everyday gamers** keeping a favourite moment (a win, a view) | Easy, quick, looks good on the wall | "From Pixels to Prints", gaming in-jokes, "Get back to gaming" | Spec-heavy pages (gsm, low-VOC) can feel like homework |
| **Gift buyers:** partners, parents, friends buying for a gamer (the About story) | Reassurance: delivery time, quality, what arrives, returns | Plain delivery and returns answers, "ready to hang" | Jargon (HUD, 1440p, photo mode) and in-jokes they won't get; no gift-specific guidance (a gift note, getting the screenshot from someone else) |
| **Streamers and creators** printing their own art or channel moments | Professional finish, bulk or repeat orders | Precise specs, the side-by-side comparison | Personal-use framing and no bulk options |

## Questions for the review

1. Who's the **primary** audience? The voice can flex by page, but the homepage needs one.
2. How much **gaming slang**: a seasoning in headings and empty states (today), or more?
3. Is mild slang ("pretty damn good") on-brand, or too casual for gift buyers?
4. Should the site speak to **gift buyers** directly (a gift FAQ, delivery cut-offs before holidays)?

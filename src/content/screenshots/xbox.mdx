## OneDrive

OneDrive, Microsoft's cloud storage service, seamlessly connects your Xbox and PC, making it a convenient option to transfer screenshots.

1. **Enable OneDrive on Xbox:**
    - Press the Xbox button on your controller to open the guide.
    - Navigate to _‘Profile & system’_, _‘Settings’_, _‘Devices & connections’_.
    - Select _‘Media preferences’_, _‘Capture & share’_.
    - Choose _‘Open captures folder’_, and select _‘On’_ to enable automatic upload to OneDrive.
2. **Access screenshots on PC:**
    - On your PC, ensure you're signed in to the same Microsoft account used on your Xbox.
    - Visit the OneDrive website or open the OneDrive app.
    - Navigate to the _‘Screenshots’_ folder under _‘Xbox Game Bar’_.
3. **Access screenshots on your phone:**
    - Download the OneDrive app from your device's app store ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/microsoft-onedrive/id477537958 'View One Drive on the App Store'), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.skydrive 'View One Drive on the Google Play Store')).
    - Sign in with the same Microsoft account used on your Xbox.
    - Open the _‘Screenshots’_ folder under _‘Xbox Game Bar’_.

## Xbox app

The Xbox app provides a direct connection between your Xbox console, PC, and mobile device.

1. **Install the Xbox app:**
    - Download the Xbox app from the [Microsoft Store (PC)](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/xbox/9MV0B5HZVK9Z 'View the Xbox app on the Microsoft Store') or your device's app store ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/xbox/id736179781 'View the Xbox app on the App Store'), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.microsoft.xboxone.smartglass&hl 'View the Xbox app on the Google Play Store')).
    - Sign in with the Microsoft account linked to your Xbox.
2. **Access screenshots:**
    - Open the Xbox app.
    - Navigate to the _‘Capture & share’_ tab.
    - All your captured screenshots and game clips will be available here.
3. **Sharing and saving:**
    - On PC: Right-click the screenshot and choose _‘Save image as...’_.
    - On phone: Tap and hold the screenshot, then select _‘Save image’_.

## USB drive

If you prefer a wired connection, using a USB drive is a straightforward way to transfer screenshots.

1. **Prepare a USB drive:**
    - Insert a USB storage device into your Xbox's USB port.
    - In the Xbox guide, go to _‘Profile & system’_, _‘Settings’_, _‘Devices & connections’_, _‘Media Devices’_.
    - Select your USB drive and choose _‘Format storage device’_, then follow the prompts.
2. **Copy your screenshots:**
    - Navigate to _‘Profile & system’_, _‘Capture & share’_, _‘Recent captures’_.
    - Select the screenshots you want to transfer and choose _‘Copy to storage device’_.

</Section>

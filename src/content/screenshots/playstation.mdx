## PlayStation App

The PlayStation App is a handy tool for managing your PlayStation screenshots.

1. **Install the PlayStation App:**
    - Download the PlayStation App from your device's app store ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/playstation-app/id410896080 'View PlayStation App on the App Store'), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.scee.psxandroid 'View PlayStation App on the Google Play Store')).
    - Sign in with your PlayStation Network (PSN) account.
2. **Connect your console:**
    - Ensure that your PlayStation console and your device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
    - Open the PlayStation App and tap the _‘Connect to PS4/PS5’_ option.
3. **Access screenshots:**
    - In the PlayStation App, select _‘Library’_ from the menu bar.
    - All your captured screenshots will be available here.

<Callout title='Heads up'>

Screenshots are available in the PlayStation App for 14 days after creation on your PS5 console.

Learn more at [PlayStation.com](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/support/games/ps5-game-captures-ps-app/ 'How to access PS5 game captures on PlayStation App').

</Callout>

## USB drive

If you prefer a wired connection, using a USB drive is a straightforward way to transfer screenshots.

1. **Prepare a USB drive:**
    - Insert a compatible USB stick into one of your PlayStation’s USB ports.
2. **Copy your screenshots:**
    - Navigate to the _‘Capture Gallery’_ under the system’s capture settings.
    - Find the screenshots you want to transfer and press the _‘Options’_ button on your controller.
    - Choose _‘Copy to USB Storage Device’_ and follow the on-screen prompts.

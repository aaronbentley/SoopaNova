## The honest bit

Making and shipping anything has a footprint, and we're not going to pretend otherwise: we don't claim to be carbon neutral, and we can't put a carbon number on your print. What we can do is make it only when you want it, close to home, and built to last.

Our prints are made by our print partner, Prodigi. Their [sustainability page](https://www.prodigi.com/sustainability/) has more on how they work.

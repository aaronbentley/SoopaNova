<Section>

## What products do you offer?

Art prints, canvases, framed prints and framed canvases, each made to order. You can choose the frame colour or how a canvas's edges wrap, and see the details of each one when you create your print.

</Section>

<Section>

## What sizes can I print?

Sizes run from {sizeRange}, depending on the product and where we're delivering to.

When you upload a screenshot, we grade each size by how sharp it will print. Sizes too big for your screenshot's resolution can't be ordered.

</Section>

<Section>

## How long will it take to receive my print?

Every print is made to order and usually sent out within one to five working days, depending on the product (framed canvases take longest). Standard delivery then takes around 2–3 working days in the UK, 4–6 in the US and 5–7 in the EU. Express is quicker: you'll see the options and their prices at checkout. We'll email you when your print's on its way, with tracking where the courier provides it.

</Section>

<Section>

## Can I cancel my order?

Yes, for 90 minutes after you place it. Choose **Cancel order** next to it on your [orders page](/orders/) and we'll refund you in full. After that, it's on its way into production and can't be cancelled.

</Section>

<Section>

## Where are you able to ship to?

The United Kingdom, the 27 countries of the European Union and the United States.

</Section>

<Section>

## Who does your printing?

Our print partner, Prodigi. Your print is made and posted from one of their labs, usually in the market it's going to: the UK, the Netherlands or the US.

</Section>

<Section>

## What if something's wrong with my print?

If your print arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we'll replace it or refund you. The same goes if your order hasn't arrived 30 days after you placed it. Every print is made to order just for you, so we can't take it back if you simply change your mind.

Email [support@soopanova.app](mailto:support@soopanova.app) with your order ref and photos of the problem (and the packaging, if it was damaged in the post) and we'll put it right. Our [Terms](/terms/) have the details.

</Section>

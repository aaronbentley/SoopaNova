<Section>

## Context

This Privacy Policy explains how SoopaNova ("we") handles your personal information when you use our website and order prints. We're responsible for that information as its data controller.

</Section>

<Section>

## Information we collect

### Your account

Your email address. If you sign in with Google, Discord or Microsoft, we also receive your name and profile picture from them.

### Your screenshots

The screenshots you upload. They're deleted automatically after 3 days. We keep a small preview image with each order so you can see it on your orders page.

### Your orders

What you ordered and paid, your delivery name, address, email and phone number (which you enter at checkout), your delivery's progress and tracking, and your confirmation that the print is for personal use, with when you gave it.

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

### Visits to our website

Anonymous statistics about the pages visited and how quickly they load, including the type of browser and device and the country. These don't use cookies or identify you.

</Section>

<Section>

## How we use your information

- To run your account and sign you in
- To make and deliver your prints, email you about your order, and help with any problems with it
- To check uploads automatically against our content rules, and keep the website secure
- To keep the records our accounts and taxes need
- To see how the website is used, so we can improve it

We use your information to fulfil our contract with you (your account and orders), to meet our legal obligations (our records), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service safe and improving it. We don't send marketing emails, and we never sell your information.

</Section>

<Section>

## Who we share it with

Only the services we use to run SoopaNova, each for its own part of the job:

- [Clerk](https://clerk.com/legal/privacy 'Clerk Privacy Policy'): accounts and sign-in
- [Google Firebase and Cloud Vision](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy 'Google Firebase and Cloud Vision Privacy Policy'): storing your uploads and orders, and checking uploads against our content rules
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy 'Stripe Privacy Policy'): payments
- [Prodigi](https://www.prodigi.com/privacy-and-cookie-policy/ 'Prodigi Privacy Policy'): printing and delivering your order (with their couriers), so they receive your screenshot and delivery details
- [Resend](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy 'Resend Privacy Policy'): sending emails about your order, so they receive your email address and order details
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy 'Vercel Privacy Policy'): hosting the website, the country you connect from (for prices in your currency) and anonymous visitor statistics
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/ 'Cloudflare Privacy Policy'): protecting sign-in and sign-up from bots

Some of these services store or process information in the United States, under safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

</Section>

<Section>

## How long we keep it

- Uploaded screenshots: 3 days
- Your account: until you delete it
- Orders and their preview images: as long as our accounts and taxes need them, even if you delete your account

</Section>

<Section>

## Cookies and browser storage

We only use what the website needs to work: cookies and browser storage that keep you signed in, a cookie with the country you're connecting from (worked out by our host from your IP address, so we can show prices in your currency), and browser storage that remembers your theme and delivery country. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout page.

</Section>

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can delete your account yourself on your [account page](/account/), and contact us about anything else.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (UK) or your local data protection authority.

</Section>
